Body & chassis safety requirements — draft input for bulk conversion.

1. When E2E_Lock_Request becomes Unlock_Pressed, E2E_Door_Lock_State must become Unlocked within 800 ms.
2. E2E_Door_Lock_State must never become Unlocked while E2E_Gear_Position is Drive.
3. E2E_Wiper_Mode must never become Off while E2E_Rain_Detected is Raining.
4. E2E_Window_Motion must stay Blocked for 3000 ms.
5. E2E_Hood_State is always Closed.
6. When E2E_Lock_Request becomes Trunk_Release, E2E_Trunk_State must become Open within 2000 ms.
7. E2E_Brake_Pedal must never become Released while E2E_Gear_Position is Reverse and E2E_Vehicle_Speed is 0.
